# Privacy Policy

**Social Media Video Automation**  
Last updated: September 21, 2026

## 1. Overview

Social Media Video Automation is a tool used to manage and publish video content to social media accounts authorized by the user, including TikTok accounts.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, and handled when using Social Media Video Automation.

## 2. Information We Access

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may access information provided through TikTok's APIs and authorized scopes, including:

- TikTok user ID or Open ID
- Basic profile information, such as display name and profile image
- Authorization information required to access TikTok's APIs
- Information necessary to upload and publish video content

The application only requests access necessary to provide its intended functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used to:

- Authenticate authorized TikTok accounts
- Identify the TikTok account connected to the application
- Upload video content
- Publish video content to authorized accounts
- Check the processing or publishing status of submitted content

Information obtained through TikTok is not sold or used for advertising.

## 4. Authentication and Credentials

Social Media Video Automation does not request or store TikTok account passwords.

TikTok authentication is performed through TikTok's official authorization system. The application may store authorization tokens necessary to access TikTok APIs on behalf of an authorized user.

Authorization tokens are used only for application functionality permitted by the user.

## 5. Information Sharing

Information obtained through TikTok is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by TikTok as necessary to provide TikTok authentication, API access, video uploading, and publishing functionality.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application and maintain authorized TikTok integrations.

Authorization information may be removed when an account is disconnected or access is revoked.

## 7. User Control

Users may revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through applicable TikTok account and application settings.

After authorization is revoked, the application will no longer be able to access TikTok APIs on behalf of that authorization.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops, its functionality changes, or applicable platform requirements change.

## 10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the application's official project repository.
